# Original SPOTlight fitout assets

`maintenance_workbench.glb` and `bridge_hoist.glb` are original geometry created for SPOTlight. No downloaded model or texture is included in either asset.

These two assets are dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The dedication applies only to these assets; existing third-party models retain their documented licenses.

Source: `art/spot-room-fitout.blend`. Rebuild with `scripts/build-room-fitout.py` in Blender. Dimensions and geometry budgets are recorded in `manifest.json`.
